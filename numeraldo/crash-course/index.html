How it's built

Numeracy first.

Numeraldo is a numeracy organization, so this course refuses to hand you a rule without showing the arithmetic underneath it. Every module has a worked example you can check.

Do the math

Every claim is checkable

When we say bank fees cost $824 a year or that waiting five years to invest costs $134,000, the line of arithmetic is printed right there. Disagree with an assumption and you can redo it.

Interactive

Calculators, not just formulas

Compound growth, the true cost of a credit card minimum payment, a monthly fixed-income worksheet, and a red-flag checker for a call that feels wrong. They run in your browser, and nothing is uploaded, stored, or sent.

Accessible

Large type, printable, free

The seniors track ships in larger type with a size control. Both tracks print cleanly for classroom or community-center handouts. No account, no email, no paywall.

For teachers and facilitators

Use it, copy it, teach from it.

These pages were written to be taught from as much as read alone.

  • Each module stands on its own: a 15-minute session can be a single module, and the syllabus links jump straight to it.
  • The Print button in the header produces a clean handout: navigation and calculators drop out, and quiz answers print alongside the questions for an answer key.
  • The quizzes and checklists need no grading: answers appear on click, and checklists remember themselves on the reader's own device.
  • Numeraldo delivers both tracks as free in-person workshops. Schools, libraries, senior centers, and community organizations are all welcome to ask.
