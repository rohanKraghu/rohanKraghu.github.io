Our mission

Numeracy is the literacy nobody screens for.

Reading gets tested, tutored, and funded. The ability to handle a number — an interest rate, a dosage, a paycheck, a ballot measure — is assumed. Numeraldo treats it as a skill that can be taught at any age, and builds programs that do exactly that.

Numeracy, n.

The ability to use, interpret, and express quantitative ideas in order to understand and manage daily, public, and professional situations.

Who we serve
Seniors

Financial security and independence

Reading a statement, spotting a scam, understanding a fixed-income budget. Workshops designed for people who were never taught this and are too often asked to trust someone else with it.

Youth

Academic preparation and analytical thinking

Weekly small-group instruction that rebuilds arithmetic fluency, then pushes into the estimation, proportion, and data reasoning that standardized tests and adult life both demand.

Underserved communities

Economic empowerment and upward mobility

Calculators, curriculum, and instruction delivered where cost is the barrier — so a missing $130 device never decides who takes the advanced class.

Partnership

The Casio Calculator Access Initiative

Active partnership Est. 2026 Device donation + training

Numeraldo has partnered with Casio to close the calculator gap. Casio supplies scientific and graphing calculators at no cost to the student; Numeraldo handles distribution, and — the part that usually gets skipped — teaches students how to actually use the machine.

  • 01Calculators placed directly with students through our partner sites, not warehoused.
  • 02A two-session onboarding: the keys that matter, and the ones that quietly cost you points.
  • 03Classroom sets loaned to teachers running units that require a graphing device.

Weekly instruction

Numeracy Club at the Boys & Girls Club of Lower Bergen County

Every week Tuesdays · 4:00–5:15 pm Grades 4–8

Numeraldo teaches at the Boys & Girls Club of Lower Bergen County every week during the school year. Sessions are small-group and hands-on: no lecture, no worksheets handed out and collected, just students doing math out loud until the number sense sticks.

  • 01Mental math and estimation drills to open — fast, competitive, loud.
  • 02A real-world block: unit pricing, percentages, sports statistics, budgets.
  • 03Homework support for whatever students walked in the door carrying.

Free online course

The Financial Literacy Crash Course

Open to everyone Two tracks · 16 modules No sign-up

Two self-paced courses — one written for teenagers, one for seniors — covering the money decisions each group actually faces. Every module shows the arithmetic underneath the rule, because a number you can check is a number you can argue with.

  • 01Teens — paychecks, bank fees, budgeting, compound interest, credit, paying for college, investing, scams.
  • 02Seniors — fixed-income budgeting, reading statements, safe interest, withdrawals, scam recognition, who to call.
  • 03Calculators, quizzes, and a 47-term glossary. Printable for classroom and community-center handouts.
