Founder

Numeraldo · 501(c)(3) nonprofit

Founded a numeracy education nonprofit on a simple observation: the people who struggle most with numbers are rarely the people offered help with them.

  • Built the organization from scratch — programs, curriculum, partnerships, and 501(c)(3) structure.
  • Negotiated a corporate partnership with Casio to supply scientific and graphing calculators at no cost to students.
  • Teach weekly small-group sessions personally, rather than delegating the classroom work.
  • Publish a free two-track financial literacy course used by teens, seniors, and the families who sit down with them.

Honest backtesting framework

Planned · next up Python

An event-driven backtester built around point-in-time data, explicit slippage and fee models, and walk-forward validation. The interesting output is the negative result: showing a strategy's Sharpe degrade as realistic costs are layered in and look-ahead bias is removed. Understanding why backtests lie is the part worth demonstrating.

repo link once it's up

ML on astronomical survey data

Planned Python · ML

Exoplanet transit detection and variable-star classification on TESS and Kepler light curves, using public data from MAST. Severe class imbalance and noisy, irregularly sampled time series make it a genuine modelling problem rather than a tutorial dataset.
