Honest backtester

A trading-strategy simulator built to catch its own false positives. It starts from the comfortable version of a strategy (Sharpe 6.94) and removes five flattering assumptions one at a time until the same strategy finishes at −0.19. Letting the model see tomorrow's prices accounted for 94% of the edge by itself.

Open source · 347 tests Python · NumPy · event-driven, point-in-time data
04

Finding planets in starlight

A planet crossing in front of its star dims the light by a fraction of a percent, on a schedule. This pipeline flattens each light curve, searches it for that repeating dip, then scores 23 measurements of every candidate with a gradient-boosted classifier. Planets are 4% of the data, so it is judged on average precision: 0.745, against 0.04 for guessing and 0.18 for the best single measurement.
