03

Honest backtesting framework

An event-driven backtester built on point-in-time data, explicit slippage and fee models, and walk-forward validation. The headline result is a negative one: the same strategy degrades from Sharpe 6.94 to −0.19 as five comfortable assumptions are removed one at a time. Look-ahead bias alone accounts for 94% of the apparent edge.

View the repo →
Live · 115 tests Python · NumPy · event-driven, no pandas in the hot path
04

Exoplanet transit detection

Detrending, box-least-squares search, and 23 vetting statistics into a gradient-boosted classifier, on light curves with a 4% planet rate. Average precision 0.73 against a chance rate of 0.04 — 18× chance and 4× the best single-statistic baseline. Scored on average precision, not accuracy, because accuracy is meaningless at a 4% positive rate.

View the repo →
Live · 86 tests Python · scikit-learn · NumPy · SciPy
05

Retirement projection tool

A compound-interest and retirement projection tool for the people who attend Numeraldo workshops — the 70+ already walked through Roth IRA planning are the first users. Results in real dollars as well as nominal, because the inflation-adjusted number is the one that decides anything.

About

I started Numeraldo because the people who struggle most with numbers are rarely the people offered help with them.

That has turned out to be the same instinct behind everything else I work on. In the Strano Lab it meant writing the pipelines that made catalyst data legible rather than waiting for someone else to. At a hedge fund it meant building the back-testing framework from scratch, and learning quickly that the interesting question is not what a backtest claims but why it lies. At Numeraldo it means teaching the session myself every week, not delegating the classroom. I study mathematics and artificial intelligence at MIT starting this August, and I'm looking for work where shipping the thing is part of the job.
